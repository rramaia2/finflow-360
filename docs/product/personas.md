# User Personas

## Customer

Submits payments and tracks their status.

Permissions:

- Create a payment
- View their own payments
- View payment-status history

## Partner System

Uses machine-to-machine authentication to submit payment requests.

Permissions:

- Create payments
- Retrieve payments submitted by that partner

## Operations Analyst

Investigates processing and settlement failures.

Permissions:

- Search payments
- View technical failure information
- Retry eligible transactions
- Review reconciliation exceptions

## Auditor

Reviews transaction and administrative activity.

Permissions:

- Read payment-status history
- Read audit history
- Export compliance reports
- Cannot modify transactions

## Administrator

Manages roles and operational configuration.

Permissions:

- Manage user roles
- Manage operational settings
- View platform health
